Software Training That Actually Sticks

Proven methodology that ensures fast, accurate software adoption without productivity loss.

The Problem with Most Software Training

New system rollouts and onboarding fail because training focuses on features, not actual workflows. Users complete courses but still can't perform their jobs effectively.

What Real Users Have Taught Me

Over 100 software training packages and 5,000+ pieces of direct learner feedback have taught me what separates successful software adoption from expensive, time-consuming failure. The insight: people don't need to know everything about the software — they need to complete their specific tasks quickly and accurately.

The Guide, Test, Reference Method

  1. Guide

    First the learner needs to know how to…

    Deliver only the essential information at the moment of need. No overwhelming feature dumps or "nice to know" content. Just the exact steps required for each process in their role.

  2. Test

    Then we need to ensure they understand…

    Verify learners can complete real work processes independently and accurately. Not multiple-choice questions about features — actual task completion in close-to-live systems.

  3. Reference

    Lastly they need the tools to succeed long-term…

    Quick-access support materials for ongoing use. Remembering every step isn't the goal; getting work done efficiently is.

Proven at Scale

On Europe's largest corporate system transfer, I led a project and personally created over 60 SAP training modules using this methodology, enabling thousands of users to transition to new processes without productivity loss.

The Result

Fast, accurate software adoption that drives business continuity, not disruption.
